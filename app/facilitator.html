Room and round

Rectangular field (millimetres). Level radar at the origin, facing +Y. Confirm the left/right sign with a marked-point walk test; use Mirror X only if needed. Changing these settings invalidates the setup check.

Default: 3 m wide × 2 m deep, Y 1.5–3.5 m. Mark START and VAULT at the positions shown on the dashboard. These are radar-plane estimates, not foot tracking. Do not pitch the sensor downward and assume a floor projection.

Keep the navigator behind the radar. Verify coverage before checking the box. Check the LED direction patterns with the updated firmware before play.

Camera Wi-Fi setup
Connect your phone to the camera setup network, then open http://192.168.4.1 to enter venue credentials. Public sharing stays off.
Use a 2.4 GHz personal network or phone hotspot. This form sends credentials over the local USB connection and stores them on the ESP32, not in the source files. Campus enterprise Wi-Fi is not implemented.
Restarting the camera during an attempt invalidates it. Attach the supplied XIAO antenna before enabling Wi-Fi. The PC Toktik service remains the game authority.
