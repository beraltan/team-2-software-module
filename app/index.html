Guide the infiltrator

Player faces the camera so they can see its LED. Left is screen-right (+X); right is screen-left (−X). Forward is toward the camera (−Y); back is away (+Y). Hold to signal; release to stop.

Requested signal: STOP

Flashing amber means tracking or connection fault. Practise signals first. Keep facing the camera while stepping; mark the orientation on the floor.
